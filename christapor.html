Christapor Yaacoubian

Founder and Creative Director

Christapor Yaacoubian is a writer/director and the founder of wontbetold Productions.

Raised in an immigrant family in Western Sydney, he writes from lived experience. Meurants Lane, his debut as playwright and director, was shortlisted for Sydney Theatre Company’s Patrick White Playwrights’ Award in 2022, sold out its season at La Mama Theatre in 2024, and won Excellence in New Writing at the Dionysus Awards in 2025.

Once an award-winning actor, he performed on stages in Sydney, Melbourne, New York, LA and Vancouver. He no longer acts, and now focuses on writing and directing.

He is now developing Meurants Lane as a feature film, which he will write and direct, with the proof of concept complete. Through wontbetold Productions he also makes commercials and music videos.
