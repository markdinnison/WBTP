Meurants Lane

Adapted from the award-winning stage play Meurants Lane: A Western Sydney Wog Story.

Starring Elias Anton, Arthur Giamalidis and Juan Fernando Monge.

  • Elias Anton: Logie winner (Most Outstanding Newcomer, Barracuda) and AACTA nominee for Best Lead Actor (Of an Age). Also Australia Day opposite Bryan Brown, Swift Street (SBS), War Machine (Netflix) and The Airport Chaplain (SBS/Netflix), led by Hugo Weaving.
  • Arthur Giamalidis: Swift Street (SBS). Recently shot Fog City.
  • Juan Fernando Monge: All Her Fault (NBCUniversal), A Royal in Paradise and La Brea (NBCUniversal).
