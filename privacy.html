This site does not currently run analytics or set its own cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including using reputable providers and limiting who can see it. No online system is completely secure.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry or the project it relates to, and then delete them.
